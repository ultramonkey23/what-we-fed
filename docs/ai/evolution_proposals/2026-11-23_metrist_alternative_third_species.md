## Design summary (Metrist)
1. Trigger: rhythmic regularity measured as jitter over recent inter-pulse
   intervals; the trigger is comfort (a groove you stop thinking about).
   Silence and bursts are irrelevant. High jitter/rubato breaks the lock.
2. Response: timing displacement, never speed scaling (orbit speed fixed);
   when locked, the Downbeat lands one offbeat past your pulse; when the
   pattern breaks, the strike drifts back ONTO your pulse slot (trade/parry).
   Timing windows unaltered (timing truth); no lane/radius crossing (spatial
   purity/fair lane readability); single parameter (phase offset), sequential
   mutation.
3. Telegraph (one warning): the Ghost Pulse — a pale after-image of your own
   most recent shot ignites in the empty slot between two beats, one beat
   early. Readable rule: "the echo fires in the gap, not on the beat."
